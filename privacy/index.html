Your information helps the town stay connected. Here is what is processed, why, and how you stay in control.

01 /

The short version

Mafia is a multiplayer social deduction game provided by Yermek Seidualy (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how personal information is handled when you use the iOS or Android app, its multiplayer features, this website, or contact us.

You choose a display name rather than registering with an email address or password. Multiplayer play still involves data: room activity, messages, an optional avatar, and audio when you use voice chat.

02 /

Information we process

  • Player profile: your chosen display name, a generated profile identifier, and an optional avatar. Your name, preferences, profile identifier, and room membership are stored on your device. Anonymous authentication identifiers and access tokens are also processed to authenticate requests to our services.
  • Multiplayer activity: room codes and identifiers, player identifiers, room settings, readiness, assigned roles, night actions, votes, game state, and timestamps are processed to run and synchronize matches.
  • Text chat: messages, player names and identifiers, room identifiers, and message timestamps are sent to our backend so participants can see the discussion.
  • Avatar images: an image you select is resized and stored locally and uploaded to cloud storage. Uploaded avatars have public URLs; anyone with the URL may be able to view the image. Do not upload sensitive images.
  • Voice chat: when your microphone is enabled, microphone audio is transmitted to participants in your voice channel through LiveKit software hosted on servers managed by us at livekit.mafiaparty.click. Room and player identifiers, anonymous authentication credentials, temporary voice-access tokens, IP addresses, and technical connection information are processed to authenticate participants and provide voice communication.
  • Purchases on iOS: Apple processes payments. The iOS app uses StoreKit purchase and entitlement information to determine Premium access and restore purchases. We do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Support and technical data: information you send by email, and connection or service logs processed by our backend, server-hosting, and communication infrastructure, may include IP addresses, device or network information, timestamps, and errors.
03 /

Why we use it

We use information to create and join rooms, identify and authenticate players, deliver chat and voice, synchronize gameplay, display avatars, remember preferences, provide purchased features, answer requests, troubleshoot faults, and protect the service from misuse.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing necessary to deliver the game is based on our agreement with you; security and service operation rely on legitimate interests; legal compliance relies on legal obligations; and optional processing that requires consent relies on your consent. You can withdraw consent without affecting processing already lawfully carried out.

04 /

Who receives information

Other room participants receive the profile and gameplay information needed for the match, including chat and voice you share. A room code is an invitation mechanism, not a guarantee of confidentiality. Participants may copy or record what you share.

Providers may process data in countries other than yours, under their applicable agreements and safeguards. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or threats to others’ rights.

05 /

Your controls

  • Change your display name or remove your avatar in the app’s Settings. Removing an avatar also requests deletion of its cloud copy.
  • Mute your microphone in the game or revoke microphone access in your device’s iOS or Android settings. Transmitting your voice requires microphone permission.
  • Choose which photo to share using the system photo picker. Selecting an avatar does not give the app unrestricted access to your photo library.
  • Leave a room or stop using the app. Removing the app does not automatically delete cloud records or cancel an Apple subscription.
  • Manage sound, haptics, and language in the app.

The current apps do not include an audio-recording feature, advertising SDK, or a request for precise device location. Our self-hosted voice infrastructure and server-hosting provider process technical connection data needed to operate and secure voice chat. Other participants may still record what they hear using their own devices.

06 /

Retention & security

Information is kept for the purposes described above, including operating rooms, resolving requests, security, and applicable legal requirements. The app requests deletion of chat messages when a room is reset and of relevant room/player records when a room is dissolved. These actions do not guarantee immediate removal from provider logs, caches, backups, or other participants’ devices.

Local preferences remain until cleared or removed with app data. Cloud avatars remain until replaced or a deletion request is completed. There is no single fixed deletion deadline for every data type; retention depends on the record, service operation, provider settings, and legal requirements.

No internet service or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information in names, avatars, voice, or messages.

07 /

Requests & privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, restrict, or object to processing of your information, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data-protection authority.

Email support@mafiaparty.click with your request. To help locate game records, include your display name and the relevant room code and approximate date if available. Do not send passwords or payment-card details. We may need additional information to verify a request; because profiles do not use email sign-in, we may be unable to identify some records from an email address alone.

08 /

Younger players

Follow the age rating displayed for Mafia in the app store for your region. If you are not old enough to consent to the relevant data processing or enter an agreement under local law, a parent or guardian must be involved where required. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately should contact us to request review and deletion where applicable.

09 /

This website & changes

These legal pages do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or a contact form. The hosting service may process request logs and essential technical information to deliver and protect the website. External links lead to services with their own privacy practices.

We may update this policy as the game or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision; additional notice or consent will be provided where required.
