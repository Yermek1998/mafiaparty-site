Rooms & connection

I can’t join a room.

Check the room code with the host and confirm that the room is still available and has space. Check your internet connection, then try again. Free hosts can create rooms for up to 12 players; Premium hosts can create rooms for up to 25.

The game is not updating or I disconnected.

Check your connection and reopen the app. If the room is still active, try returning to it. If the problem continues, email us with the room code, the approximate time, and what you saw. Avoid sharing a room code publicly.

03 /

Voice chat

Other players can’t hear me.

Check that your microphone is not muted in Mafia. In iPhone Settings, find Mafia and allow microphone access if the option is shown. Check your headset or Bluetooth audio connection and your internet connection. Whether you can speak may also depend on the current game phase.

I can’t hear other players.

Check your device volume and selected audio output. Disconnect an unused Bluetooth headset if sound is being routed to it. Ask another player to check their mute status, then check your connection.

04 /

Premium & purchases

I paid for Premium, but it is not active.

Confirm that Apple completed the purchase and that you are using the Apple Account that made it. Open Mafia’s Settings, open the Premium screen, and use the restore-purchases option. If access is still missing, contact us with the plan and purchase date. Never send your Apple password or full payment-card details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Mafia, then choose the cancellation option. Deleting Mafia does not cancel your subscription. Follow Apple’s cancellation instructions if you need help.

How do I request a refund?

For purchases billed by Apple, submit your request through Apple’s purchase support. Refund eligibility is determined through Apple’s process and applicable law.

05 /

Report a bug or a player

For a technical issue, include:

  • Your iPhone model, iOS version, and Mafia app version, if available.
  • What you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The approximate date and time, and a room code if relevant.
  • A screenshot if it helps, with unrelated personal information hidden.

To report harassment, inappropriate content, or suspected cheating, describe the incident and include the relevant display name, room code, and approximate time. Send reports privately to support@mafiaparty.click.

06 /

Profile & privacy requests

You can change your display name and remove your avatar in the app’s Settings. For requests about access to or deletion of cloud data, email support@mafiaparty.click. Include your display name and relevant room details so we can try to identify the records.

Deleting the app does not automatically delete cloud data or cancel a subscription. Learn more in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
