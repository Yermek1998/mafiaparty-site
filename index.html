How do I join my friends?

Ask the host for the room code, then choose Join room in Mafia. The host can create the room and choose its roles before the game starts.

Does everyone need Premium?

Room capacity and additional role options depend on the host’s Premium status. Free hosts can create rooms for up to 12 players; Premium hosts can create rooms for up to 25.

Can we talk during the game?

Mafia supports voice and text chat. Microphone permission is needed to speak, and your ability to speak may depend on the game phase.

What is the minimum age?

You must be at least 13 years old to use Mafia. Follow the age rating in your App Store region and the eligibility requirements in our Terms of Use.

Where can I get help?

Visit our Support page for room, audio, and Premium troubleshooting, or email ermek.seidualy@gmail.com.
